Pearls

  • Prep smoothness improves reading
  • Seatability is checked before cement
  • Block rather than overcut when appropriate
  • Document IDS

Pitfalls

  • copying a crown prep for every onlay
  • long thin beveled glass ceramic
  • saliva hidden in scan
  • temporary cement left under the final bond

Say it back

Teach-back: describe the case-specific path from removal of old material to lab handoff. Application: explain when you would stop rather than take a scan.

Open items for this module

  • Complete, validated IDS reactivation method for your chosen system is NOT supplied — select and document one before relying on IDS.
  • Impression set/disinfection and temporary-material times are not verified here — use each product’s own IFU.
  • Standalone Adhese Universal IFU was read on a third-party mirror; confirm the current US/lot version.
