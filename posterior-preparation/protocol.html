Step by step

Bold values come from the IFUs coded beside them (see the source table). Highlighted rows are checkpoints — don’t pass them until they’re met.

  1. Confirm diagnosis, consent, planned coverage and material. Record occlusion and shade before isolation.
  2. Establish isolation; protect adjacent tooth/soft tissue. Remove failed material and manage caries according to the pulpal plan. Re-evaluate support and cracks.
  3. Prepare selected inlay/onlay/overlay, preserving useful enamel and using rounded internal transitions. Verify exact Press form/technique diagram I01 and reduction index; do not widen solely for symmetry.
  4. Checkpoint: sufficient ceramic space, supported margins, clear insertion path, no uncontrolled fluid, no unresolved pulp exposure. If not met, stop/replan.
  5. If proceeding with an appropriately trained Adhese-based sealing plan: I05 selective enamel etch 15–30 s (or chosen etch-and-rinse with dentin 10–15 s), rinse ≥5 s; scrub adhesive ≥20 s, air-thin immobile, cure 10 s at 500–1,300 mW/cm². Seal prepared dentin; follow the selected complete IDS method for oxygen-inhibited layer, record compatibility and future reactivation. This card does not invent those missing system-specific steps.
  6. Inspect/finish preparation; record scan or impression with all margins, adjacent/opposing teeth and bite. Impression set/disinfection time = selected material’s IFU, not a generic course number.
  7. Place removable compatible provisional with no eugenol inhibition, no locking undercuts, open margins or high occlusion. Use its actual IFU timing. Do not substitute the definitive e.max as a temporary.
  8. Send lab the material, coverage map, thickness/occlusion goals, records, stump/target shade and IDS history; request intaglio pretreatment history.
Stop / pitfallsdeep unrecordable margin, impression defects, voided scan, guesswork clearance, pooled adhesive, damaged adjacent tooth, unknown temporary compatibility. Bench/supervised rehearsal is required before first independent clinical cases.
