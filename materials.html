Parameter ledger — exact product and source

I01 — IPS e.max Press

Full official IFU, 2026-01-08 / Rev. 2: https://www.ivoclar.com/en_us/eifu/download/67261 . English preparation table, PDF page 4; extracted lines 232–327.

Staining/monolithic technique minimum restoration thicknesses, not blanket bur-depth prescriptions:

  • Inlay/onlay: fissure depth 1.0 mm and isthmus width 1.0 mm.
  • Occlusal veneer/table top and partial crown: 1.0 mm in listed dimensions.
  • Thin veneer: 0.3 mm circular and 0.4 mm incisal.
  • Veneer: 0.6 mm circular and 0.7 mm incisal.
  • No sharp internal edges; rounded internal shoulder and/or pronounced chamfer. Bonding mandatory for these partial-coverage/veneer forms.
  • These are minimums, not an instruction to cut every tooth that amount. Space is measured from the approved final contour; the lab may require more for masking, anatomy or technique. Layered/cut-back designs have different tables. Do not reproduce one chart as all e.max ceramics.

I02 — Monobond Etch & Prime

Official IFU, 2024-02-26 / Rev. 0: https://www.ivoclar.com/de_de/eifu/download/104822 . English application, PDF page 1.

  • After try-in, rinse and dry restoration extraorally.
  • Agitate onto bonding surface with light pressure 20 s; allow another 40 s reaction.
  • Rinse until green is removed; dry with oil-/moisture-free air about 10 s.
  • If residue persists in microporosities, water ultrasonic cleaning up to 5 min is described.
  • Avoid contamination. Do not add an unnecessary HF step or stack another silane on top without an instructed reason.

I03 — HF + Monobond Plus route

Official Monobond Plus IFU, 2023-11-16 / Rev. 0: https://www.ivoclar.com/en_us/eifu/download/98102 . Official IPS Ceramic Etching Gel IFU (2016-09 Rev.1, the version served by the official endpoint): https://www.ivoclar.com/en_us/eifu/download/136288 . Contemporary cement IFUs repeat these glass-ceramic parameters.

  • IPS e.max CAD/Press: 20 s with specified ~5% HF product, then thoroughly rinse/dry.
  • IPS Empress glass-ceramic: 60 s, then rinse/dry. Do not transfer 60 s to e.max.
  • Monobond Plus: thin coat, react 60 s, disperse excess with strong air.
  • HF and Monobond Etch & Prime are alternative conditioning pathways, not sequential defaults.
  • Zirconia: no HF/Etch & Prime; use its precise air-abrasion/primer IFU. No universal pressure is provided for all zirconia. Do not use phosphoric acid as zirconia cleaner.
  • Acid-resistant PPE, protected extraoral work zone, appropriate neutralization/waste, written exposure-response plan; exposure requires immediate copious rinsing and urgent medical assessment. Absence of immediate pain does not exclude HF injury.

I04 — Ivoclean

Official IFU, 2025-05-23 / Rev. 0: https://www.ivoclar.com/en_us/eifu/download/66129 . Rinse/dry after try-in, shake bottle, cover restoration bonding surface, allow 20 s, thoroughly rinse/dry, then reprime. If contaminated again, repeat specified surface treatment. Extraoral only; do not apply to the tooth or assume it treats silicone-paste contamination.

I05 — Adhese Universal + etching

Official LC/DC cement IFUs below explicitly describe this paired adhesive protocol. The more recent standalone manufacturer IFU, dated 2025-01-28 WW Rev.2, was opened at https://www.topdental.eu/sub/topdental.eu/images/A_navody-1/ivoclar/Adhese_Universal_eIfU_WW_Rev2.pdf (third-party host; exact US/lot version still requires lookup).

  • Selective enamel phosphoric acid etch: 15–30 s, rinse vigorously at least 5 s.
  • If choosing etch-and-rinse: enamel 15–30 s, dentin 10–15 s, then rinse ≥5 s. Do not desiccate dentin.
  • Scrub Adhese Universal at least 20 s; air-disperse to glossy immobile film, no pools; light-cure 10 s at 500–1,300 mW/cm² for the standard protocol used here. Ensure complete coverage.
  • Do not transfer the specialized 3 s high-power posterior Class I/II protocol to veneers, indirect delivery, deep-pulp situations or curing through silicone.
  • The standalone PDF header says “dual-curing” but the description and application say light-curing; product page and paired cement IFUs also identify the ordinary product as light-curing. Do not use the anomalous header to justify leaving it uncured.
  • Standalone IFU warns peroxide bleaching/disinfectants within ≤14 days may inhibit polymerization. No universal same-day whitening + bonding protocol is endorsed.

I06 — Variolink Esthetic LC

Official IFU, 2025-09-11 Rev.1: https://www.ivoclar.com/en_us/eifu/download/67303 . Suitable glass-ceramic/lithium-disilicate/composite restorations <2 mm with sufficient translucency. After secure seating, optional tack cure: 2 s, light ≥500 mW/cm² at 10–15 mm, moving around the cement line; not final cure. Remove excess promptly, hold restoration securely, cover margins with glycerine gel.

Final cure per mm ceramic and per segment, starting proximal margins:

  • 500–800 mW/cm²: 20 s.
  • 900–1,300 mW/cm²: 10 s.

Respect both cement and curing-light IFUs; do not extrapolate a missing output range or high-intensity “3 s cure.” Cure all relevant surfaces/overlapping fields. Keep guide close and perpendicular for final cure; tack-cure distance is not final-cure positioning. A single 10 s flash is not a full veneer delivery protocol.

I07 — Variolink Esthetic DC

Official IFU, 2023-11-13 Rev.0: https://www.ivoclar.com/en_us/eifu/download/67313 . Self-cure working/total cure time: at room temperature about 5/8 min; intraoral 37°C about 2/4 min, starting after extrusion (ambient light and temperature affect times). Optional tack cure 2 s per quarter surface, distance max 10 mm. Final light-cure table per mm ceramic/segment is 20 s at 500–800 mW/cm² or 10 s at 900–1,300 mW/cm². Protect margins with glycerine after cleanup. Do not confuse the 4 min intraoral total self-cure with extra waiting time after a 2 min work period. Clinical opaque cases require the appropriate adhesive and current compatible product instructions.

I08 — IPS Empress Direct

Manufacturer IFU dated 2025-01-30 Rev.0, opened at third-party mirror: https://www.topdental.eu/sub/topdental.eu/images/A_navody-1/ivoclar/IFU_IPSEmpress_Direct.pdf . Direct veneers explicitly indicated.

  • Enamel/general increments max 2 mm; Dentin shades max 1.5 mm.
  • Per increment, 20 s at 500–900 mW/cm², or 10 s at 1,000–1,300 mW/cm².
  • Additional cure if tip positioning was poor; overlap fields if restoration is larger than tip. Finish/check occlusion/polish. Opal not for occlusal reconstruction.
  • This establishes a freehand/direct-layering pathway, not an injection-through-index cure schedule.

I09 — Injection molding limitation (do not hide this)

Ivoclar’s official case article describes Tetric EvoFlow, two clear matrices, alternate-tooth isolation, Adhese Universal and a case-specific 10 s/40 s total light sequence. A clinical blog is not a universal IFU and its cure sequence cannot be generalized across shades, matrix thicknesses and curing lights. Reviewed Tetric EvoFlow PDF is historical and does not verify a current definitive full-veneer injection protocol. The course must show technique principles but flag a clinical hold until exact current US material/index IFUs and a validated through-index curing schedule are available. A printable bench-rehearsal protocol can be supplied now; a falsely “verified next-day definitive injection” protocol cannot.
