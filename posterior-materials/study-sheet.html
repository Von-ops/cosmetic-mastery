The one-page logic

Inlay = no cusp coverage.

Onlay = selected cusp coverage.

Overlay = whole occlusal table.

Direct composite is a genuine comparator.

Lithium disilicate and leucite are etchable glass ceramics.

Zirconia is not.

Lab Press and lab CAD are both valid workflows.

Poor isolation is a stop, not a cement-selection problem.

Pearls

  • Diagnose before prescribing
  • Use exact product name
  • Preserve useful enamel
  • Have the lab confirm surface treatment

Pitfalls

  • e.max means zirconia
  • every MOD means crown
  • stronger ceramic compensates for saliva
  • veneer cement shades solve all masking problems

Say it back

Application: give a 60-second material/coverage prescription for a first case, and one finding that would change it.
