Step by step

Bold values come from the IFUs coded beside them (see the source table). Highlighted rows are checkpoints — don’t pass them until they’re met.

A. Supported direct-composite veneer pathway (competent clinician/supervised first cases)

  1. Select hydrated shade, approve contour/function and diagnose disease/occlusal risk. Protect neighboring teeth; isolate.
  2. Prepare only as required by the additive/restorative plan. Establish palatal/proximal guide if useful; do not remove healthy enamel just to make the veneer “look prepared.”
  3. With Adhese Universal pathway I05: enamel phosphoric acid 15–30 s, rinse ≥5 s; if selected etch-and-rinse includes dentin, limit dentin 10–15 s; scrub adhesive ≥20 s, air-thin immobile, light 10 s at 500–1,300 mW/cm².
  4. Place and adapt IPS Empress Direct in planned layers. Max 2 mm generally; 1.5 mm Dentin shades. Build shell/contact/body/enamel deliberately.
  5. Cure each permitted increment under I08: 20 s at 500–900 or 10 s at 1,000–1,300 mW/cm², with overlapping fields and additional exposures when positioning was compromised. No 3 s veneer extrapolation.
  6. Finish contours/line angles, independent contacts and cleanable margins; check occlusion/phonetics; polish to high gloss and document baseline. Checkpoint: no joined teeth, voided margins, retained excess or traumatic guidance.

B. Injection-molding rehearsal—not a clinically cleared definitive protocol yet

⚠ Bench / model rehearsal onlyDo not perform this branch on a patient until the clinical hold below is lifted.
  1. On a model, verify wax-up and fabricate two stable clear indices using the selected silicone’s actual mix/set IFU; no invented universal set time.
  2. Trial support/seating/vent access; make injection openings without retained silicone plugs. Mark and isolate alternating teeth.
  3. Rehearse seating and progressive injection without air entrapment or movement. Practice removal, excess access and independent contacts.
  4. Clinical hold: obtain current US Tetric EvoFlow full-veneer indication, shade/increment limits and validated composite/index/light exposure schedule. The article’s 10 s/40 s case sequence is not promoted as a universal IFU.
  5. Only after those requirements and hands-on competency are met should this be converted into a patient-specific definitive protocol, with timed bonding and complete prescribed through-index/after-index cures.
Stop / pitfallsincomplete product validation, uncontrolled fluid, rocking/bubbled index, uncertain light path, inability to finish proximal excess, unresolved function. This honest hold is preferable to a falsely practice-ready protocol.
