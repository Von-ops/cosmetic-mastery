⚠ Clinical hold — injection moldingThe Tetric EvoFlow full-veneer injection branch is bench/model rehearsal only. A current US full-veneer indication and a validated through-index cure schedule have not been verified. The supported Ivoclar chairside route today is IPS Empress Direct layering. Hold details

The one-page logic

Design transfer is not material validation.

Wax-up/index/independent teeth/light transmission all matter.

Ivoclar direct-layering route is supported; full-veneer EvoFlow injection parameters still require current US IFU confirmation.

PowerFlow is not the default anterior substitute.

No unsupported equivalence to porcelain.

Numbers that matter (IFU-sourced)

Minimums and times exactly as found in the opened IFUs. Re-open the current US IFU for your lot before clinical use.

Pearls

  • Test fit before etching
  • Separate adjacent teeth
  • Planned contours reduce finishing

Pitfalls

  • retained silicone in tip
  • unstable index
  • partial cure hidden by glossy surface
  • joined contacts
  • oversized cervical emergence

Say it back

Teach-back: explain the difference between “copies the shape well” and “a validated definitive material protocol.” Application: demonstrate an index trial on a model and identify one remaking criterion.

Open items for this module
