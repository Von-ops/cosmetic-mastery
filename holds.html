Clinical holds & verification status

What is verified, what is not, and what must be confirmed before a protocol is used on a patient.

⚠ Primary clinical hold — injection-molded full veneersIvoclar publishes an injection-molding case using Tetric EvoFlow, but a current US full-veneer indication and a validated through-index cure schedule were not verified. Module 6’s injection branch is labeled bench/model rehearsal. Lift the hold only after you have (1) the current US Tetric EvoFlow IFU with an explicit indication, (2) shade/increment limits, (3) a validated composite/index/light exposure schedule in writing, and (4) supervised hands-on competence.

Verified

  • 14 Ivoclar product families on official Ivoclar pages.
  • 7 full IFUs on official Ivoclar hosts (e.max Press, Monobond Etch & Prime, Monobond Plus, IPS Ceramic Etching Gel, Ivoclean, Variolink Esthetic LC, Variolink Esthetic DC).
  • 2 manufacturer IFUs read on third-party mirrors (IPS Empress Direct; standalone Adhese Universal) — confirm US/lot versions.
  • 15 PubMed-indexed references; 6 curated videos.

Still needed before clinical finalization

  • Current US, lot-specific Adhese Universal, IPS Empress Direct and Tetric EvoFlow IFUs.
  • Through-index injection cure validation and full-veneer indication.
  • Your selected clear-PVS index, mock-up, impression and provisional-material IFUs.
  • A complete validated IDS reactivation protocol for your chosen system.
  • A manufacturer-permitted cement for opaque/thick veneers (LC-ineligible).
  • A good dedicated contemporary e.max posterior-preparation video.
  • Hands-on supervised competence — a passed quiz is not credentialing.
