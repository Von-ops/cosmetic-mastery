Pearls

  • Photograph before dehydration
  • Approve design before cutting
  • Separate shade and geometry problems

Pitfalls

  • digital preview treated as consent
  • bulky no-prep
  • one bleach shade for every substrate
  • marketing case reports as randomized evidence

Say it back

Teach-back: give the patient a plain-language comparison of direct composite and ceramic without promising permanence. Application: define one mock-up change that would alter your preparation plan.

Open items for this module

  • Mock-up/index resin mix, set and removal times are not verified here — use the selected material’s IFU.
  • The ≤ 14-day peroxide warning comes from the standalone Adhese IFU on a third-party mirror; confirm with the current US IFU.
  • Printed/milled resin “veneers” are not taught as definitive ceramic equivalents.
