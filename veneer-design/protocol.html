Armamentarium & setup

  • camera/consent, shade tabs, retraction, periodontal/restorative records, appropriate radiographs, scan/impression/bite capability, diagnostic design, index and selected removable mock-up resin IFUs
  • articulating media, mirror, photo/phonetic checklist
  • Ivoclar candidates: e.max Press for lab ceramics
  • Empress Direct for direct restorations
  • No generic “mock-up set time” is substituted for the actual material

Step by step

Bold values come from the IFUs coded beside them (see the source table). Highlighted rows are checkpoints — don’t pass them until they’re met.

  1. Record patient’s main complaint and rank desired changes. Discuss options and irreversible/maintenance risks.
  2. Diagnose disease, pulp/periodontium, parafunction and occlusal stability. Checkpoint: active disease/untreated bruxism or complex function is resolved/referred before veneer commitments.
  3. Record hydrated shade and photographs; capture full-arch/opposing/bite records. Document major existing restorations and intended bond substrate.
  4. Prescribe additive-first lab design with proposed edge position, contours, contacts, texture and shade/masking strategy.
  5. Try the approved design with a suitable removable mock-up/index; use its measured mix/set/removal time from its IFU. Do not bond the entire mock-up as a definitive veneer by accident.
  6. Evaluate rest/full smile, F/V/S sounds, lip support, cleansability and static/excursive function. Invite patient feedback and photograph revisions.
  7. Approve design/material/space before preparation; record the laboratory’s fabrication technique and exact thickness chart. I01 Press monolithic thin veneer: 0.3-mm circular/0.4-mm incisal minimum restoration dimensions; conventional veneer 0.6/0.7 mm. These are not mandatory tissue removal values.
  8. Plan isolation, bonding route and delivery optics. If bleaching is part of treatment, obey the actual peroxide/adhesive interval rather than bonding immediately.
Stop / pitfallsexpectation mismatch, untested speech/function, unconsented photos, active inflammation, wrong shade under dehydration. This is a planning/mock-up protocol, not permission to cut an unapproved case tomorrow.
