Lab e.max partial-coverage delivery

Back to lesson
Competency gateSupervised rehearsal first. This card is a chairside reference once competence and case suitability are established — it does not make a new operator independent overnight.

Armamentarium & setup

  • confirmed lab material/pretreatment
  • current IFUs I02–I07
  • rubber dam/clamps/floss ligatures where appropriate, retractor/suction
  • oil-/fluoride-free cleaning paste
  • try-in paste
  • dedicated ceramic well/holding aid
  • timer
  • either Etch & Prime or indicated HF + Monobond Plus
  • Ivoclean when its branch is indicated
  • Total Etch
  • Adhese Universal
  • selected LC/DC cement
  • clean checked compatible light/protective shield
  • glycerine gel
  • floss/finishing strips/ceramic polishers
  • occlusal media

Step by step

Bold values come from the IFUs coded beside them (see the source table). Highlighted rows are checkpoints — don’t pass them until they’re met.

  1. Remove provisional and residues, rinse/dry without overdrying. Confirm diagnosis and no new symptoms needing reassessment.
  2. Try in carefully; verify full seat, contacts, margins/shade. Adjust/re-polish or return if needed; do not force an unbonded ceramic into occlusion.
  3. Establish reliable isolation. Checkpoint: no visible contamination or unresolved misfit.
  4. Extraoral known e.max route: rinse/dry → Etch & Prime scrub 20 s, wait 40 s, rinse until green gone, air ~10 s. Alternative specified HF route: e.max 20 s → rinse/dry → Monobond Plus 60 s → air. Do not combine routes routinely. For documented pretreatment use the corresponding cleaning/repriming branch; Ivoclean, when indicated, 20 s, rinse/dry, reprime.
  5. Tooth lane: if no separate IDS reactivation plan applies, selective enamel etch 15–30 s, rinse ≥5 s; Adhese Universal scrub ≥20 s, air to immobile glossy film, light 10 s at 500–1,300 mW/cm². No pools. Manage contamination by the product instructions and fresh applicator.
  6. Apply chosen cement to restoration and seat under stable pressure. For DC, respect about 2 min intraoral working time / 4 min total self-cure from extrusion; do not start the clock late. LC only if I06 eligibility is satisfied.
  7. Clean excess while stable. Optional DC tack 2 s/quarter, max 10 mm; LC circular tack 2 s, 10–15 mm, ≥500 mW/cm². Avoid locking in proximal excess or pulling restoration free with floss.
  8. Glycerine at margins; final cure per mm ceramic and segment: 20 s at 500–800 or 10 s at 900–1,300 mW/cm², all accessible needed fields. Respect light IFU and use close/perpendicular positioning for final exposure.
  9. Rinse gel; inspect/finish cement and ceramic; floss; remove isolation; check static/excursive occlusion and re-polish adjusted ceramic. Document baseline and advise reporting pain, high bite or loosening.
Stop / pitfallsunknown substrate, persistent fluid, nonseating restoration, incompatible adhesive/cement, no valid light output. This protocol does not authorize HF exposure without staff training/PPE or independent first cases without competence.
