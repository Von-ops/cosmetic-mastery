The one-page logic

  1. Identify
  2. Clean/try-in
  3. Isolate
  4. Restoration route
  5. Tooth route
  6. Compatible cement
  7. Stable seat/cleanup
  8. Glycerine/final segmented cure
  9. Finish/check/document

Contamination resets the applicable protocol.

No bond is stronger than its weakest interface.

Pearls

  • Surface history in hand before tray setup
  • One conditioning route
  • Timer visible
  • Record curing output/exposure

Pitfalls

  • HF on tooth/zirconia
  • uncured tooth adhesive
  • thin pooled film mistaken for dryness
  • early final cure trapping cement

Say it back

Teach-back: call out both lanes as if directing an assistant. Application: give your response to a lab form that says only “ceramic, pretreated.”

Open items for this module

  • Zirconia conditioning parameters are not given here — follow the specific zirconia maker’s IFU.
  • The standalone Adhese Universal PDF header says “dual-curing” while its text says light-curing; this course follows the official paired cement IFUs (light-cure separately).
  • IPS Ceramic Etching Gel IFU is the 2016 Rev. 1 version served by the official endpoint.
  • Complete IDS reactivation method still to be selected for your system.
