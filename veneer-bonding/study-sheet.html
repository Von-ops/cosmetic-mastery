The one-page logic

  1. Labels/IFUs
  2. Remove temporary/inspect
  3. Try-in/approve
  4. Reliable isolation
  5. Correct ceramic branch
  6. Correct substrate branch
  7. Eligible cement
  8. Seat/hold/clear
  9. Glycerine + segmented cure
  10. Polish/function/document
  11. Maintenance

If cement eligibility fails, stop instead of improvising.

Pearls

  • Identify each tooth and pretreatment
  • Patient optical approval first
  • Maintain seating pressure during cleanup

Pitfalls

  • dehydrated shade misjudgment
  • treating tack cure as final
  • locked contacts
  • pooling uncured adhesive
  • bonding a misfit
  • residual subgingival cement

Say it back

Teach-back: give your assistant a precise delivery time-out. Application: identify your plan if the lab reports pre-etching but cannot identify the ceramic or primer.

Open items for this module

  • Opaque or ≥ 2 mm veneers: a manufacturer-permitted alternative to LC is NOT verified (the reviewed DC IFU types table does not list veneers). Stop and confirm before bonding such a case.
  • Adhese Universal standalone-IFU header anomaly — cure it separately per the official cement IFUs.
  • Try-in paste and any curing-light-specific outputs must follow their own IFUs.
