Study sheet — Porcelain veneers: controlled space, retained enamel

Take quizBack to lesson

The one-page logic

  1. Design approved
  2. Hydrated shade
  3. Isolate/protect
  4. Depth controlled by final contour
  5. Check cervical/middle/incisal planes
  6. Choose margin/contact/incisal form
  7. Map substrate
  8. Seal if selected
  9. Clean records
  10. Removable provisional
  11. Explicit lab Rx

Pearls

  • Preserve bondable enamel
  • Index before impressions
  • Return to approved design when uncertain

Pitfalls

  • uniform flat-plane reduction
  • unnecessary deep margins
  • aggressive shade-change rules
  • unplanned tissue sacrifice
  • assuming the lab will fill every inadequate-space problem with stronger ceramic

Say it back

Teach-back: explain why planned final contour, not a uniform bur pass, controls your prep. Application: design the contact/incisal approach for a single peg lateral versus a tooth with an old large Class IV; state what extra information you need.

Open items for this module

  • IDS reactivation method and provisional material/cement IFUs still need selecting.
  • V04 dimensions are demonstration-specific; your lab and I01 govern.
